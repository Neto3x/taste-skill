# Taste Skill v2 — Anti-Slop Art Direction

## Core Objective
Enforce high-end art direction and eliminate generic, templated, or "AI-made" design patterns (slop) across all web components and pages.

## Design Rules & House Style
1. **Anti-Slop Aesthetics:** Avoid oversized rounded corners, unnecessary neon gradient blobs, generic card layouts, and predictable hero sections.
2. **Typography & Hierarchy:** Use high-contrast font pairings with distinct scale relationships and deliberate line heights.
3. **Color & Palette:** Use restrained, intentional color schemes with clear hierarchy and high-contrast accessibility compliance.
4. **Composition & Layout:** Prioritize grid alignment, negative space, and content-driven spacing instead of arbitrary padding.
5. **Quality Gate:** Every component generated must look custom, premium, and award-level before implementation.
